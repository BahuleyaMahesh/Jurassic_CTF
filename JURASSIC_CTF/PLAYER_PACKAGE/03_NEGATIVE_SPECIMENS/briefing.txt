STAGE 03 — THE NEGATIVE SPECIMENS
====================================================================
Category   : Image Processing / Digital Forensics
Difficulty : Medium

BRIEFING:
Four laboratory imaging plates from Containment Wing B were corrupted or
processed on darkroom/microscope hardware prior to the shutdown.
Each plate requires a distinct pixel-level correction:
  - specimen_01.png : Color inversion (photographic negative)
  - specimen_02.png : Extreme brightness/contrast recovery
  - specimen_03.png : Single RGB channel isolation
  - specimen_04.png : Gamma correction / intensity thresholding

FILES:
  - specimen_01.png, specimen_02.png, specimen_03.png, specimen_04.png

OBJECTIVE:
Recover the specimen ID, vault code, and sublevel bay. Flag format:
  JURASSIC[<SPECIMEN_ID>_<VAULT>_<BAY>]

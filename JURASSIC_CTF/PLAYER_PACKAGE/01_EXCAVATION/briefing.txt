STAGE 01 — THE EXCAVATION
====================================================================
Category   : Image Forensics / Steganography
Difficulty : Easy

BRIEFING:
Recovered from a waterproof Pelican case outside Brackett City, these
two field photographs were logged days before the July 18, 1997 incident.
Investigate the JPEG metadata and inspect the raw pixel channels of
the amber lab plate to uncover the excavation sector and coordinates.

FILES:

  - excavation_02.png

OBJECTIVE:
Recover the Sector ID, geographic coordinates, and calibration note
required to unlock Stage 02. Submit the Stage 01 flag:
  JURASSIC[<SECTOR>_<COORDINATES>]
